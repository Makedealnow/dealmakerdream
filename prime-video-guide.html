Prime Video Without Prime: What the Standalone Plan Really Costs

You don't need a full Prime membership to watch Prime Video. Amazon sells it on its own for $8.99/month — but that's not the whole bill for most people. Ads are included in that price, removing them costs extra, and the channels inside the app are separate subscriptions. Here's the full picture before you sign up.

Standalone or full Prime — which one?

  • Only want to watch? Standalone at $8.99/month is the cheaper door in — just budget $11.98 if you hate ads.
  • Order from Amazon even a few times a month? Full Prime at $14.99 adds free fast delivery, Prime Music, Prime Reading, and member deals on top of the same video library. The $6 difference disappears fast.
  • 18–24 or a student? Don't buy either before checking Prime for Young Adults — six months free, then $7.49/month for full Prime including video.

The trap is inside the app: the most common surprise Prime Video charge isn't the membership — it's a channel. Channels offer their own free trials inside Prime Video, each converts to its own monthly charge, and each must be cancelled separately (in Prime Video's Account & Settings → Subscriptions). One membership cancel does not sweep them away. If you see an unfamiliar Amazon digital charge, check your channel subscriptions first.

How to cancel

  1. Go to amazon.com, sign in, and open Account & Lists → Memberships & Subscriptions (for a standalone plan), or Prime Membership if your video comes with Prime.
  2. Choose to end the membership and keep confirming past the offers to stay.
  3. Access runs to the end of the current billing period. Then cancel any channels separately under Prime Video's subscription settings if you added any.

Common questions

Is Prime Video free with Prime?
Yes — the core streaming library is included with a Prime membership. What still costs extra, even for Prime members: channel subscriptions, rentals, purchases, and the $2.99/month ad-free upgrade.

Why was I charged $8.99 (or another amount) by Amazon digital?
Usually one of three things: a standalone Prime Video plan you started, a channel trial that converted, or a rental/purchase. Your Memberships & Subscriptions page shows which.

Can I share Prime Video?
With full Prime, some benefits can be shared through Amazon Household. A standalone Prime Video plan is for your own account.
