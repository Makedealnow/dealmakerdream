Audible Gift Memberships: The Audiobook Gift That Can't Auto-Renew

An Audible gift membership is the rare subscription gift with no strings: you pay once, they get a credit for an audiobook every month of the gift — and it does not auto-renew, in Audible's own words, "so there are no subscription surprises." Buying for someone who already has Audible?

Still works — and works well. When a current member redeems a gift membership, they receive all of the gift's credits at once, and their existing plan is not extended or interrupted. A 6-month gift to a member is really "6 extra books, right now."

The one thing to warn your recipient about

Credits expire 12 months after they're issued. Gift credits follow the same rule as regular Audible credits. For the 12-month gift especially, the recipient should spend credits steadily rather than saving them all for the end — anything unspent at the 12-month mark is lost. Books already bought with credits are kept forever.

After the gift ends

Nothing happens — that's the point. The membership stops at the end of the gift period. If your recipient wants to keep going, they can start their own membership (our free trial guide explains that option) or take the annual plan. Nobody is charged for the transition because there's no card attached to a gift.

Common questions

Can I gift a single book instead?
Yes — on any audiobook's page, choose "Give as gift." A gift membership is usually better value when you're not sure which book they'd pick.

Do I need to be an Audible member to give one?
No. Anyone with an Amazon account can buy a gift membership.

Is the 12-month gift the same price as the annual plan?
Nearly — $150 as a gift versus $149.50 for the annual plan bought directly. The 50-cent difference buys the no-auto-renew gift format.

What if they don't redeem it right away?
They can redeem when ready; the membership starts at redemption. Just remember the credits' 12-month clock runs from when they're issued.
