Prime for Young Adults: The 6-Month Free Trial, Explained

If you're 18–24 — or a student at any age — Amazon will give you Prime free for six months, then at half price. It's the most generous trial Amazon offers, and most eligible people never hear about it because the sign-up page defaults to the regular 30-day trial. Here's who qualifies, what you actually get, and the catches.

How it works

Verify your age (18–24) or your student status when you sign up, and the six-month trial starts. Everything in regular Prime is switched on, plus Grubhub+ (free food delivery) and 5% back on eligible purchases in categories like beauty, apparel, and electronics. When the trial ends, the membership continues automatically at $7.49/month — half price — on the card on your account, unless you cancel first.

Know before you start

  • It ends. Join through age verification and the plan converts to regular full-price Prime when you turn 25. Join through student verification and it converts after four years or when your studies end. Amazon tells you the deal up front — just don't be surprised by the change.
  • No sharing. Unlike regular Prime, you can't share these benefits with another adult through Amazon Household.
  • A few benefits are trimmed during the trial itself — for example, the Prime Gaming Twitch subscription is limited to one month during the trial period.

The trap is the same as every trial: six free months feels so far away that everyone forgets. The membership auto-renews at $7.49/month when the trial ends. Set a reminder for the last month of your trial the day you sign up, and cancel in two minutes if you don't want to continue (steps are the same as our Prime free trial guide — cancelling during the trial keeps your benefits until the trial ends and costs nothing).

Common questions

I'm 26 but a student. Do I qualify?
Yes — eligibility is 18–24 or a verified higher-education student. Student status is the door for anyone older.

Is this the old Prime Student?
Yes — Prime Student grew into Prime for Young Adults, opening it to all 18–24-year-olds, not just students.

What if I already have regular Prime?
If you're eligible, switching gets you the trial and the half-price rate. Amazon handles the change at sign-up and refunds any eligible unused portion of your previous plan.

Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — online, in about two minutes, exactly like regular Prime. Cancel during the trial and you're charged nothing.
