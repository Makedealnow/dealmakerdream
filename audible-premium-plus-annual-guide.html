Audible Premium Plus Annual: Is $149.50 for 12 Credits Worth It?

Audible's annual plan gives you all 12 credits on day one for $149.50 — about $12.46 per audiobook instead of $14.95 a month on the monthly plan. For steady listeners it's the cheapest way to buy Audible books. But there are two ways people lose credits on this plan, and both are avoidable once you know them. (Audible is in the process of renaming Premium Plus to simply "Premium" — same plan, same benefits.)

Who this plan is for — and who it isn't

  • Worth it if you reliably listen to a book a month or more and you pay for Audible anyway. You get the discount and the freedom to spend credits whenever a book you want appears, instead of waiting for the monthly credit to land.
  • Skip it if your listening comes in bursts. Credits expire 12 months after they're issued, so a slow year means paying for books you never picked. The monthly plan — which can be paused — fits uneven listeners better.
  • Heavy listeners: the 24-credit annual plan ($229.50, about $9.56 per credit) is cheaper per book still, but doubles the amount riding on you actually listening.

The two traps: (1) Expiry — credits die 12 months after issue whether you've used them or not. (2) Cancellation — if you cancel with credits unspent, those credits vanish with the membership; and unlike monthly plans, an annual plan can't be paused to buy time. The fix for both is the same: spend every credit before the year runs out or before you cancel — books bought with credits are yours permanently.

How to cancel (and keep what you paid for)

  1. First, spend your credits. Pick books even if you'll listen later — once bought, they're yours forever.
  2. On a computer or phone browser, go to audible.com, sign in, and open Account Details. (Use the website; deleting the app doesn't cancel anything.)
  3. Under your membership details, choose Cancel membership and keep confirming past the alternative offers.
  4. Confirm the cancellation email arrives. Your Plus Catalog access runs to the end of the paid year; your purchased books stay in your library.

Common questions

How much do I actually save versus monthly?
Monthly Premium Plus is $14.95 × 12 = $179.40 a year for the same 12 credits. The annual plan is $149.50 — about $29.90 less, or roughly two free books a year.

What happens to my books if I cancel?
Nothing. Books purchased with credits (or cash) are yours to keep and listen to forever. You lose the streaming Plus Catalog and any unspent credits.

Can I pause an annual plan like the monthly one?
No. Pausing is a feature of eligible monthly plans. On the annual plan your options are to use the year or cancel — another reason to spend credits early rather than late.

Is there a free trial of the annual plan?
The standard Audible trial is of the monthly plan (see our Audible free trial guide). You can switch to annual afterwards if it suits you — check what Amazon offers your account at the time.
