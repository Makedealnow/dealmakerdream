Not sure? Three quick checks

1. A card with your name on it — job, marketplace, or Medicare? Insurance door.

2. No card, paying for care yourself? Cash door.

3. A card from a public program — Medicaid or CHIP? Public program door. Both Medicare and Medicaid? Start there too — your strongest protections live in that lane.

Your facts stay yours. No account to create, nothing to upload. Everything you write down exists in exactly one place — your hands.
