Watch Katie first — she explains the three lanes and how this kit walks. About a minute.

Three lanes. One of them is yours. Pick it, and the kit walks your road from there.

How your care gets paid for decides which rulebook applies — so the whole kit starts by placing you. One question does most of it: do you have a health insurance card with your name on it? The three doors below are the three answers.

Which lane are you in?

I have health insurance Through a job, bought it myself, or Medicare. Your first step: learn your plan — what it covers, and what it doesn't. I pay cash — no insurance No plan, between plans, or paying cash for this care anyway. You're owed a price in writing before anyone starts. Medicaid or a public program covers me Medicaid under any state name, CHIP for your children — or Medicare and Medicaid together.

Not sure? Three quick checks

1. A card with your name on it, from a job, a marketplace, or Medicare? That's the insurance door.

2. No card, and you'd pay for care yourself? That's the cash door.

3. A card from a public program — Medicaid, whatever your state calls it, or CHIP? That's the public program door. Both Medicare and Medicaid? Start there too — that lane holds your strongest protections.

Your facts stay yours. There is no account to create and nothing to upload. You place yourself, from your own papers, and everything you write down exists in exactly one place — your hands.

Inside your lane, you'll learn one simple way of working, and the kit's helper is explained once, right where you first need her. After your lane's setup, every road merges — the doctor, the visit, everything after, and the bill — with lane notes only where a rule actually changes for you.
