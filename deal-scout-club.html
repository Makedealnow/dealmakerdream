Deal Scout Club

A free club with one simple idea: when you find a genuine deal, you pass it forward — and Deal Scout makes sure it's real before anyone else hears about it.

How it works

  1. You find something. A clearance price, a closeout, a sale that's actually worth it. You tell Deal Scout three things: what it is, where you found it, and how much it was.
  2. Deal Scout checks it. Every find is verified against the seller's current price and availability before it goes anywhere. If it doesn't check out, it doesn't get passed on.
  3. Verified finds get posted here for the whole club — seller, price, and the date it was checked. When a price dies, the find comes down.

The Deal Scout check

No find reaches this page until it passes every step:

  • The seller and item are identified exactly — no vague "on sale somewhere."
  • The seller's live page is re-checked the same day: price and availability confirmed.
  • The price is genuinely good — below the normal price, not a fake "was" price.
  • No retailer's photos are used — club finds are text only, or Deal Scout's own artwork.
  • Every find carries the line: Prices and offers subject to change. Check the seller's current price and terms before buying.

Club rules

  • Members never post directly — everything goes through the check first. That's what keeps the club clean.
  • No member affiliate links, ever. A link you submit is used only to check your find; it is never published. Nobody profits from a find they submit.
  • The club is not a store. Nothing is bought or sold here. The club passes on checked information — you always buy from the seller, on the seller's site, under the seller's terms.
  • If MakeDealNow ever earns anything from a link on a club page, that will be disclosed on this page, in plain sight.
