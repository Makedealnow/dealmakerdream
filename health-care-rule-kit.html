The Health Care Rule Kit

Health care has rules. This kit hands you the rulebook — in order, in plain English.

Deal with hospitals and health plans as a customer with rights — not a captive.

What this kit is

  • Three lanes — one is yours: insured · cash-pay · Medicaid or state coverage. Pick your lane at the front door; you see only your road.
  • A Katie video on top of every page. Watch and listen — the words stay below as the read option.
  • Every step in three beats: DO THIS · SAY THIS (the exact words) · KEEP THIS PAPER.
  • Know Your Plan lookup: type your plan's name — nothing personal — and get its official numbers. Save the photo; that photo is your plan card.
  • The desk play, the referral play, the bill play — what to do when things go wrong, including the deviation letter.
  • Nine printable worksheets — plan card, care team, visit log, bill audit, deviation letter, and lane worksheets.
  • An AI helper (Monica), introduced once, always your choice — every step also has a paper route. Your documents are never sent to us.

What's inside

  • Front door — pick your lane.
  • Stage 1 — Know Your Plan (insured): the lookup, your plan card, the two questions.
  • The Cash-Pay Lane — the price in writing first, the good-faith estimate, the federal dispute at $400+ over.
  • Medicaid or State Coverage — renewals, notices, deadlines, and your billing protections.
  • Stage 3 — Your Doctor — find one, or verify the one you have, for this plan year.
  • Stage 4 — The Visit — opens with the desk play: your card vs. the office's screen.
  • Stage 5 — Everything After — the referral play, the three-check rule, your surprise-bill shield.
  • Stage 6 — The Bill — the itemized audit, the deviation letter, regulators before attorneys.
  • Using Your AI — the helper method, taught once: what to paste, what to cover, why the paper wins.

Who it's for

Anyone in any of the three lanes — which is everyone. Watch a short video, follow three plain beats. Works before anything goes wrong, and after a bad bill arrives.

  • No promised results. Nothing here guarantees a charge struck, a bill reduced, or any outcome. Plans and providers decide their cases; this kit covers the rules, the checks, and the order — not their decisions.
  • Support = product questions only. Access trouble, page problems, billing. We can't advise on individual cases.
