The Health Care Rule Kit

Health care has rules. This kit hands you the rulebook — in order, in plain English.

Deal with hospitals and health plans as a customer with rights — not a captive.

A guided walk through your whole health care road — knowing your plan, finding your doctor, the visit, everything after, and the bill — with Katie's video leading every page, so you can watch and listen instead of reading. The words stay on every page too, for when you want them.

The problem

Most people meet the health care system the way they meet weather: it happens to them. A card gets handed over at a desk. A referral disappears into a gap nobody owns. A bill arrives that nobody can explain — and the amount assumes you'll just pay it.

Underneath all of it sits a rulebook: what your plan must tell you, what a provider must do before you're charged, what a hospital's own paperwork has to get right. The system counts on you not knowing it. When something goes wrong, the leverage isn't arguing louder — it's auditing their compliance with their rules, and knowing which door to knock on next.

That's what this kit teaches, one step at a time, before you need it — and it's built to be reached for again when a bill goes wrong.

What this kit is

A set of guided pages — not a file dumped on you — that walks your road in the order life actually happens:

  • Three lanes, one of them yours. Insured (through a job, bought it yourself, or Medicare) · paying cash with no insurance · Medicaid or state coverage. You pick your lane at the front door, and the kit shows you only your road — with lane notes only where a rule actually changes for you.
  • A video at the top of every page. Katie introduces each stage and explains what it is and what to watch for. Most people won't read all of it — you don't have to. The full text stays below every video as the read option.
  • Simple steps, in the kit's three beats: DO THIS · SAY THIS (the exact words, ready to read aloud) · KEEP THIS PAPER.
  • Know Your Plan, with the kit's own lookup. Type your plan's name — nothing personal, just the name printed on your card — and the kit looks up its public numbers from official plan files: deductible, out-of-pocket maximum, copays. Save a photo of the result; that photo is your plan card.
  • The desk play, the referral play, the bill play. What to do when the office says "we don't take that insurance," when a referral vanishes into the gap, and when the bill arrives — including the deviation letter that hands their own broken process back to them, in writing.
  • Nine printable worksheets — the plan card, care team, visit log, episode planner, bill audit, deviation letter, and lane worksheets — because paper is part of the product.
  • A helper, introduced once, always your choice. The kit teaches you exact questions to ask an AI helper about your own paperwork — and every step offers the kit's own paper route instead. Nobody is forced into a signup, and your documents are never sent to us.

What's inside

  • Front door — pick your lane; the kit places you before any stage does.
  • Stage 1 — Know Your Plan (insured lane): the lookup, your plan card, and the two questions that unlock everything after.
  • The Cash-Pay Lane — the price in writing before anyone starts, the good-faith estimate, and the federal dispute when a bill runs $400+ over it.
  • Medicaid or State Coverage — stay on the track (renewals, notices, deadlines) and keep your rights, including the billing protections this lane holds.
  • Stage 3 — Your Doctor — find one, or verify the one you have, for this plan year.

  • Using Your AI — the helper method, taught once: what to paste, what to cover first, and why the paper always wins.

Who it's for

Anyone whose care gets paid for in one of the three lanes — which is everyone. If you'd rather watch a short video and follow three plain beats than study a rulebook, this was built for you. It works before anything goes wrong, and it works after the bad bill arrives.
