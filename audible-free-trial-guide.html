How the trial actually works

When you start the trial, Audible gives you a credit. One credit buys any premium-selection audiobook, no matter its list price — so use it on something expensive you'd never pay cash for. You also get streaming access to the Plus Catalog (thousands of included audiobooks, podcasts, and originals) for as long as your membership is active.

Two things surprise people:

  • Books bought with credits are yours permanently. Cancelling does not take them away.
  • Unused credits disappear when you cancel. Spend every credit before you cancel — see the checklist below. (One exception: if your membership is billed through the Apple App Store or Google Play, those credits don't expire when you cancel.)

The trap: the trial converts to a paid $14.95/month membership on day 31, automatically, using the card on your Amazon account. It just bills. Two safeguards: Audible itself emails you a reminder at least 7 days before your trial ends — and you should still set your own phone reminder for day 25 the moment you sign up, so the decision is yours, not your inbox's.

How to cancel (about 2 minutes)

  1. On a computer or phone browser, go to audible.com and sign in. (You cannot fully cancel inside the Audible app — use the website. And if your membership is billed through Apple or Google, cancel in that store's subscription settings instead.)
  2. Open Account Details (under your name, top right).
  3. Under your membership details, choose Cancel membership.
  4. Audible will offer alternatives — a pause, a cheaper plan, a free credit to stay. Keep choosing to continue cancelling.
  5. Confirm. You'll get an email confirming the cancellation. Your trial access runs to the end of the trial period, and books you bought with credits stay in your library.

Before you cancel: the 3-minute checklist

☐ Spend your credits. Any unused credit is lost the moment the membership ends. Pick a book — even a long one you'll get to later.

☐ Check for a pause instead. If you like Audible but need a break, Account Details offers a pause on monthly plans (up to 90 days total in a 12-month period) that stops billing while keeping your credits.

☐ Confirm the email. No confirmation email means the cancel didn't finish. Go back and complete it.

Common questions

If I cancel on day 29, am I charged anything?
No. Cancel any time before the trial ends and the total cost is $0 — and the book you bought with your trial credit is still yours.

Do I lose my books if I cancel?
Books you purchased (including with credits) stay in your library forever. You lose access to the streaming Plus Catalog and any unspent credits.

Can I get the free trial again later?
Generally, no — not soon. Audible's trial offers are not available if you have taken an Audible trial or been a member within the last 12 months.

Is the trial worth it if I only want one book?
Yes — that's the best use of it. One credit on a $30+ audiobook, cancel in the first month, keep the book. Just set the day-25 reminder.
