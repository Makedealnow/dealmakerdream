How MakeDealNow Works

MakeDealNow has three main features today:

  1. Kits: Plain-English kits that walk you through a fix step by step — the Credit Dispute Kit and The Warranty Test. Each kit page starts with a free one-page checklist.
  2. Deal Scout Club: A free pay-it-forward club. Members submit deals they find, and Deal Scout checks every one against the seller's current price before it is passed on. Nothing is published unchecked, and links submitted by members are never published.
  3. Email list: Join the free list to get the Subscription-Trap Checklist, then occasional verified finds. No spam, and every email has an unsubscribe link.
