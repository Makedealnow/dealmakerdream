Amazon Business: The Free Account, What the Membership Costs, and How to Cancel

The thing most people get wrong about Amazon Business: the account itself is free — business pricing, quantity discounts, multiple users, purchasing reports. The paid part is an optional membership on top of it (now called Prime Business) that adds free fast business shipping and spending tools. Here's the real pricing, the trial trap, and how to cancel the membership while keeping the free account.

Do you even need the paid membership?

For a lot of small businesses, honestly, no. The free account already gives you business pricing, quantity discounts, extra users, and tax-exemption handling. The paid Prime Business membership mainly adds free fast shipping for the whole account, spending-visibility and guided-buying tools, and longer payment terms on some plans.

  • Sole proprietor who already has personal Prime? Duo links to it at no extra cost — that's the sweet spot.
  • Small team ordering often? Essentials at $179/year can pay for itself in shipping alone — but only if the order volume is really there.
  • Just registering to check business prices? Take the free account and skip the membership entirely.

The trap: Prime Business is billed once a year. If you start a free trial and forget it, the trial doesn't convert to a small monthly charge — it converts to the full annual fee for your tier ($179 for Essentials, $499 for Small) in a single charge. Decide before the trial ends, and set a reminder the day you start.

How to cancel the membership (the account stays free)

  1. Sign in to Amazon Business and go to Your Account.
  2. Select the Prime Business icon.
  3. Select Manage to see your plan, renewal date, and status.
  4. Select Cancel and confirm. If you're eligible, a partial refund may apply depending on the benefits used.
  5. Your free Amazon Business account — pricing, users, reports, tax exemption — stays exactly as it was. Cancelling the membership does not close the account.

Common questions

Does Amazon Business cost money?
The account, no — it's free to register and keep. Only the optional Prime Business membership is paid, and only annually.

What do I need to register?
A work email and basic company information. To speed up verification, Amazon suggests having your EIN, business license, or state documents handy if asked.

I got a charge that says B2B Prime. What is it?
That's the Prime Business membership fee — most likely a trial that converted or an annual renewal ($179 usually means Essentials). Check Your Account → Prime Business → Manage to see the plan and renewal date.

Can I cancel a trial before it charges?
Yes — cancel any time before the trial ends using the steps above and there is no charge. Because billing is annual, don't cut it close.
