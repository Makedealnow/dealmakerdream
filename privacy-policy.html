Deal Scout Club

The Deal Scout Club pages link to forms where visitors may submit a deal they found (what the item is, where they found it, and the price) or ask Deal Scout to look for something. A name or nickname and an email address are collected only if the visitor chooses to provide them — an email is used only to reply to that visitor about their submission or question. Submissions are reviewed before anything is shared, links submitted by visitors are never published, and submitted information is not sold or shared with third parties. The MakeDealNow email list is described in the Email List section of this policy.

Email List

If you join the MakeDealNow email list, we collect the email address you enter. The list is operated through Kit (Kit, Inc., formerly ConvertKit), which processes subscriptions and sends the emails on our behalf. Joining is double opt-in: you are subscribed only after you click the confirmation link in the email Kit sends you. We use your address only to send the Subscription-Trap Checklist and occasional verified finds and guides. Your address is never sold, and it is never shared with partners or advertisers. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing stops the emails. If you reply to a list email, your reply goes to our mailbox at makedealnow@outlook.com.
